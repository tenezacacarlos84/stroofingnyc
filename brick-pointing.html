Professional Brick Pointing Services in NYC

Brick pointing (also called tuckpointing or repointing) is the process of removing deteriorated mortar from masonry joints and replacing it with fresh mortar. In NYC's harsh climate, mortar joints naturally deteriorate over time — allowing water to penetrate walls, causing structural damage, mold, and costly repairs. ST Roofing & Construction LLC provides expert brick pointing services for homes and buildings across the NYC metro area.

Brick Repointing

Remove old failing mortar and pack in new mortar to restore waterproof joints. Essential for aging brick buildings.

Tuckpointing

Precision tuckpointing for a clean, finished appearance. Two-color mortar technique for historic or decorative brickwork.

Chimney Pointing

Specialized mortar repair for chimney stacks exposed to extreme weather and temperature cycles.

Parapet Wall Repair

Repoint and waterproof parapet walls on flat roof buildings to stop water infiltration at the roofline.

Signs You Need Brick Pointing

Crumbling or Missing Mortar

Visible gaps or powder falling from joints is a clear sign repointing is needed immediately.

Water Stains on Interior Walls

Water penetrating through deteriorated joints shows up as stains or efflorescence inside.

White Staining (Efflorescence)

White mineral deposits on brick surfaces indicate water is moving through the wall.

Cracks in Mortar Joints

Hairline or larger cracks allow water in and worsen with every freeze-thaw cycle.

Loose or Spalling Bricks

When mortar fails, individual bricks can become loose or begin to crack and chip.

Your Building Is 20+ Years Old

Most mortar has a lifespan of 20-30 years. Older buildings need inspection.

Why Repoint Now — Not Later

  • Stop Water Damage — Failed mortar is one of the top causes of water infiltration in NYC brick buildings.
  • Structural Protection — Proper mortar holds masonry together. Delay leads to more expensive structural repairs.
  • NYC Climate — Freeze-thaw cycles in New York accelerate mortar deterioration faster than most climates.
  • Property Value — Well-maintained masonry improves curb appeal and resale value.
  • Code Compliance — Deteriorated facades can trigger NYC DOB violations. Stay ahead of it.
