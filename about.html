About ST Roofing & Construction LLC

Care in Every Roofing Project.

ST Roofing & Construction LLC serves homeowners and property owners in the Bronx, NYC and Westchester. Carlos Tenezaca and the team work on roof replacement, roof repairs, flat roofing, waterproofing and exterior improvements.

A good project starts with understanding your roof. We discuss the existing condition, the work proposed and the materials selected so you can make an informed decision about your property.

From shingle roofing to silicone roof coatings, the details matter. Explore photos of our work, learn about our services and contact our team to discuss your next project.
