ST ROOFING / FLAT ROOFING

Flat roofing

Low-slope roofs need attention at every connection.

What to know.

A flat roof is a system: the surface, seams, penetrations, edges and drainage all work together. An inspection helps determine whether a localized repair or a larger replacement is appropriate.

Reasons to get in touch

  • Leaks near vents, drains or parapet walls
  • Open seams, worn patches or damaged membrane
  • Water remaining on the roof after rain
