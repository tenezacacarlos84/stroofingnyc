BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

Good questions.
Clear next steps.

All questions & answers →
Do I need a repair or a full replacement?

That depends on the condition of the roof, the extent of the damage and what is underneath. Share the symptoms and arrange an inspection before deciding on a scope.

What should I include in my estimate request?

Include your location, the type of roof if you know it, what you are noticing and how to reach you. You can send project photos through WhatsApp.

Can I explore financing?

Yes. Use our Financing page to access our Hearth link. Approval, rates and terms depend on the lender and your eligibility.

When can I contact ST Roofing?

Monday through Saturday we are open 24 hours. Sunday hours are 9AM–5PM Eastern. Call about urgent roof problems so we can discuss availability.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve the Bronx, Brooklyn, Queens, Manhattan, Yonkers and Westchester. Contact us with your address to confirm whether we can visit your property.

Can I send photos before an inspection?

Yes. Open WhatsApp, attach your photos and send them to our team. Photos help start the conversation but do not replace an on-site assessment.
