Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy
Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

ST Roofing & Construction LLC ("we", "us", "our") operates stroofingnyc.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website or contact us for services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information you voluntarily provide — such as your name, phone number, email address, and property address — when requesting an estimate or contacting us.
  • Usage data collected automatically via Google Analytics, including pages visited, time on site, and device type. This data is anonymous and aggregated.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to estimate requests and service inquiries
  • Contact you about your roofing or construction project
  • Improve our website and services

We never sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This data is anonymous and aggregated — we cannot identify individual visitors from Analytics data. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

5. Contact Forms & Phone Calls

When you submit a contact form or call us, your information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and provide roofing and construction services. Our contact form uses FormSubmit to process submissions and deliver them to our email. If you choose WhatsApp, your message is handled by WhatsApp. Financing links take you to Hearth, which handles financing inquiries under its own privacy policy. Please review the policies of the service you use. We use your contact details to respond to your request.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. We do not store credit card or payment information on our website. Any financial transactions are handled directly and securely.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to or deletion of any personal information we hold about you at any time. To make such a request, please contact us at:
