Complete Roof Replacement in NYC

When it's time for a new roof, you want a contractor you can trust to do the job right. ST Roofing & Construction LLC has replaced hundreds of roofs throughout the NYC metro area, earning a reputation for quality workmanship and exceptional customer service.

Our complete roof replacement process includes a thorough inspection, detailed proposal, professional installation, and final walkthrough to ensure your complete satisfaction. We use only the highest quality materials from trusted manufacturers, and our experienced installers treat your home as if it were their own.

A new roof is a significant investment, and we're committed to helping you get the most value for your money while protecting your home for decades to come.

Asphalt Shingle

Most popular choice. Affordable, durable, and available in many colors and styles to match your home.

Flat / Torch Down

Ideal for commercial buildings and modern homes. Seamless, waterproof, and long-lasting.

Metal Roofing

Premium durability with 50+ year lifespan. Energy efficient and environmentally friendly.

Architectural Shingles

Enhanced dimensional appearance with premium warranties. Premium impact-resistant options available.

What's Included in Your Roof Replacement

  • Complete Tear-Off - Removal of existing roofing materials down to the deck
  • Deck Inspection & Repairs - We replace any damaged or rotting plywood
  • Premium Underlayment - Synthetic felt or ice & water shield for superior protection
  • Quality Shingles/Materials - Top-grade materials from trusted manufacturers
  • New Flashing - Complete flashing replacement around chimneys, vents, and edges
  • Ventilation Installation - Proper intake and exhaust vents for attic ventilation
  • Complete Cleanup - We leave your property clean, including magnet sweep for nails
  • Final Inspection - Quality check and warranty documentation
