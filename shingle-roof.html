ST ROOFING / SHINGLE ROOFING

Shingle roofing

A roof system, from the deck to the ridge.

What to know.

Shingles are the visible surface, but underlayment, flashing, ventilation and roof edges also matter. We discuss the condition of the existing roof and the scope of replacement before work begins.

Reasons to get in touch

  • Missing, lifted or visibly damaged shingles
  • Leaks around chimneys or roof transitions
  • Worn roofing that may be ready for replacement
